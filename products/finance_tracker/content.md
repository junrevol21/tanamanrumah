# 50/30/20 Budget Planner 2026

## Why This Works
The 50/30/20 rule is the simplest way to take control of your money:
- 50% for Needs (rent, food, transport)
- 30% for Wants (entertainment, dining)
- 20% for Savings & Debt payoff

## Step 1: Calculate Your Monthly Income
- Write your take-home pay (after tax)
- This is your base number for all allocations

## Step 4: Automate Your Savings (20%)
- Move 20% to a separate account on payday
- Set up auto-transfer — you won't miss what you never see

## Weekly Check-in
- Every Sunday: review what you spent
- Adjust categories that are over budget
- Celebrate small wins — consistency beats perfection

## Debt Snowball Bonus
- List debts smallest to largest
- Attack the smallest while paying minimums on the rest
- Momentum builds as each debt disappears
